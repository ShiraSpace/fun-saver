🧒 Child mode in the menu: where the toggle lives

Sunshine Quest, 360px phone. Each account keeps its saved view (child or parent). In every option the child menu lists all the other children, and its bottom button is a small "view in parent mode" that saves nothing: it lives in memory until reload or switching accounts. Tap a name to switch, flip the toggles, use the bottom button, and watch both menus follow. The three options differ only in the parent menu.
