🧒 Child mode for the whole app

Jungle Quest, phone size. One «מצב הורה/ילד» switch, the same in both menus: off in the parent menu, on in the child menu. Child mode belongs to this phone, not to an account. «הגדרות כלליות» looks like «הגדרות של יעל». Tap the switch, the «להחליף ל…» rows, and the switch again.

Children on the family:
  • The parent menu as on main, minus the «מצב ילד» row under the picker. The picker only picks a child.
  • «⚙️ הגדרות כלליות» looks like «הגדרות של יעל» and holds what belongs to this phone rather than to one child.
  • One «מצב הורה/ילד» switch: off in the parent menu, on in the child menu; its knob carries 👵🏼 for parent mode and 🧒🏼 for child mode. Turning it off returns the whole app to parent mode, so «מצב הורה» is gone.
  • In the child menu the switch is a card the size of «הכסף שלי» and «מראה», at the bottom.
  • «להחליף ל…» lists every other child; each opens in child mode. Child mode survives a reload on this phone (a cookie); nothing is saved on an account.
